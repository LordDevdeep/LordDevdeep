**🏛️ [LegalMinds](https://github.com/LordDevdeep/LegalMinds)**
AI legal analysis for Indian law that doesn't hallucinate statutes.

Turns a plain-English description of a legal problem into a structured analysis — applicable laws, rights, penalties, timelines, costs, filing portals — with every citation matched against a curated database of 60+ real Indian statutes (IPC, CrPC, Consumer Protection Act, IT Act, and more). The model is *constrained* to that list, so it can't invent section numbers. Also generates court-submittable legal notices and exportable PDF reports, bilingual (English/Hindi).

`Next.js 14` `TypeScript` `Supabase` `Groq LLM`

[**Live →**](https://legalminds-sepia.vercel.app)

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

**🛰️ [InfraWatch](https://github.com/LordDevdeep/Infrawatch)**
Satellite-based illegal-construction detection for Indian municipal corporations.

Built for Google Solution Challenge 2026. Feeds satellite tiles through Gemini 2.5 Flash to flag unauthorized construction, auto-assigns an SLA tier (4–24 hrs based on confidence), routes the case to a field officer, and drafts a legal notice citing real municipal statutes — falling back to a generic reference rather than inventing a section if it can't confidently match one. Includes automatic failover to a second AI provider if Gemini rate-limits, plus a public citizen-reporting portal.

`React 18` `Express` `Gemini 2.5` `JWT Auth`

[**Live →**](https://infrawatch-sepia.vercel.app)
